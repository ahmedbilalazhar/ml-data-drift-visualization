## Decision and scope

Two extensions are admitted:

1. **Model sensitivity on the v1 synthetic task.** Compare the frozen v1 logistic model with one shallow `DecisionTreeClassifier(max_depth=6, min_samples_leaf=25, criterion="gini", splitter="best")`, using identical seed-specific train, calibration, reference and monitoring instances for seeds 100-129 and conditions C0-C6. This pairing reuses v1's declared generator seeds to hold observations constant; it is not new final evidence for the generator. Both models use training-only `StandardScaler`; each gets its own frozen correctness isotonic map and ATC-style raw-confidence threshold fitted only on the shared calibration partition. No tuning or model selection is performed.
2. **One new synthetic XOR task.** Use fresh development seeds 190001-190005 and final seeds 200-229. Generate eight independent standard-normal features; the noiseless binary concept is `1{(X0 > 0) XOR (X1 > 0)}` with independent 10% label noise. Use five predeclared conditions: X0 (stationary); X1 (irrelevant X7 mean shift +1.5 after row 4000); X2 (X0 mean shift +0.75 after row 4000); X3 (concept complement after row 4000 with inputs unchanged); and X4 (label noise increases from 0.10 to 0.35 after row 4000 with inputs unchanged). These are synthetic intervention labels with known onset by construction, not empirical onset labels. Fit both models and separate model-specific calibrators/thresholds as above.

The existing local ELEC2 stream is **rejected for v2**. Its raw ARFF fails time-order validation (date is not monotone; 528 date/period pairs repeat), so its row chronology cannot be reconstructed uniquely from the supplied timestamp fields. The processed parquet also fails to read with a Parquet repetition-level histogram error. The OpenML metadata calls the license field `Public`, but this is not treated as a more specific license grant. No natural-data claims will be made.

## Frozen protocol

- **Historical partitions:** For each synthetic seed: 4,000 training, 2,000 correctness-calibration and 2,000 historical-reference rows, generated from the task's baseline law with distinct deterministic stages. Monitoring is 12,000 rows per condition, 24 consecutive batches of 500. Preprocessing is fitted on training rows only. IDs are task-, seed-, partition- and condition-namespaced and must not overlap across historical partitions.
- **Models:** v1 logistic regression remains `C=1`, `max_iter=500`, class cutoff 0.5. The only added learner is the fixed depth-6 tree above, `random_state=seed`; no search, post-result tuning or seed exclusion. Both models retain their own reference accuracy and therefore their own realised-harm target. Harm counts across models are descriptive and are not, by themselves, a model-ranking metric. Report paired-cohort accuracy, MAE and bias as well.
- **Confidence:** Each model predicts with its frozen class-probability output. Raw AC uses `max(p, 1-p)`. Isotonic calibration targets that model's correctness on the 2,000 calibration rows and never changes its class prediction. ATC-style threshold selection uses the same calibration data, candidates including 0, observed scores, 1 and `nextafter(1,+infinity)`, and the largest threshold for exact objective ties.
- **Input evidence:** Preserve the v1 eight original-feature KS tests and three training-only PCA-coordinate tests, Holm correction over 11 at alpha 0.05 and original-only ablation recomputed over eight. Inputs and labels are identical across models; do not describe input detection as model-specific impact evidence.
- **Impact and ablations:** For each model, report raw AC, calibrated AC/IDM identity, model-specific ATC-style estimate and historical constant. Use the v1 exact realised-batch harm rule (historical accuracy minus accuracy strictly greater than 0.05), plug-in Poisson-binomial 95% interval and concern flag at 0.95. Retain calibration removal, latent-test removal and interval-to-point-rule ablations where defined. Report interval width, empirical coverage, flags, TP/FP/FN/TN counts, denominators and harmful-unflagged counts. Missing precision/recall/AUROC stays undefined.
- **Labels and clocks:** Reuse frozen predictions/proxies across delays 0, 5 and 20. Persist phase-0 predictions before phase-1 release, including delay zero; drain to clock 24+d without fake input. Online records contain no current labels or evaluator truth. Release verification and evaluator-only joins are separate.
- **Primary analyses:** Per-task, condition, model and regime: paired accuracy, raw/calibrated estimation MAE and bias, calibration error, interval coverage and width, concern counts, pre/onset/post/recovery summaries, and input alarm burden. Synthetic onset matching is reported only for the interventions whose input or label-law change is explicitly programmed. It is not reported as natural-drift precision/recall.
- **Uncertainty:** Use 2,000 deterministic paired-seed bootstrap replicates over complete seed vectors for synthetic summaries; preserve per-seed values and pair models on identical task/condition/seed/cohort. Do not count delays, models, conditions, batches or ablations as independent datasets. Any undefined metric remains missing.
- **Development/final separation:** Development uses only XOR seeds 190001-190005. Final XOR uses all seeds 200-229 with no replacements. V1-task model sensitivity uses exactly seeds 100-129 and never changes v1 files. Final seed sets are checked for collision with v1 and development.
- **Schemas:** Online batch CSV records task, seed, condition, model, cohort, batch, model-specific estimates/intervals/concern, common input evidence and timings; it has no current accuracy, labels, F1 or harm. Release CSV records availability clock, cohort, delay, IDs digest, realized accuracy/F1 and model-specific harm. Evaluator artifacts contain exact labels and joins. Caches are partitioned by task/seed/condition/model and validated against regenerated IDs.
- **Stop rules:** Abort final interpretation if any train/calibration/reference overlap, future-label leakage, model-class prediction mismatch, cache/ID mismatch, queue non-drainage, an omitted declared seed, a convergence warning, non-finite score, v1 hash change or reproduction mismatch occurs. Preserve failed outputs as failed diagnostics; do not replace seeds, thresholds or conditions. If the full extension cannot be completed within the work window, report completed evidence and the exact incomplete component.

## Outcome-informed hypotheses and mechanisms

V1 showed that isotonic calibration slightly worsened overall MAE and interval coverage; conditional-only harm was missed; calibrated flags clustered in C6; and PCA/KS dependence onset performance was limited. These findings motivate, but do not predetermine, the following tests:

- **H1:** The tree may show a different source calibration profile and confidence-based impact-estimation failure pattern than logistic regression, especially under concept change. A result in either direction is informative; no expected winner is assumed.
- **H2:** The XOR task should favor the tree's axis-aligned interaction representation over a linear logistic model under the stationary law. This is a generator-level hypothesis, not external-domain validation.
- **H3:** XOR complement and noise interventions keep the feature distribution fixed, so input-only tests should not identify their onset; model-specific correctness may deteriorate without a corresponding input alarm.
- **H4:** Any PCA advantage under dependence change is conditional on the fitted training representation and will be reported as a task-specific result.
