Abstract
We implement an impact-aware monitoring prototype connecting input alarms, descriptive feature evidence, estimated
accuracy, scheduled-label verification and a coordinated evidence view. The frozen logistic-classifier study comprises 210
paired-condition streams, 2,520,000 observations and 5,040 named batches across 30 seeds. Original-feature KS tests
are combined with three frozen PCA coordinates; correctness calibration, Average Confidence and plug-in
Poisson-binomial intervals provide a pre-label impact channel. Delays of 0, 5 and 20 batches reuse the same prediction
caches.
The equally weighted mean accuracy-estimation MAE is 0.114 [0.113, 0.115] for calibrated AC and 0.113 [0.112, 0.114]
for raw AC. These are seed-bootstrap intervals, not guarantees under arbitrary shift. The calibrated concern rule flags 169
of 5040 batches, with 160 correct flags, 9 incorrect flags and 1487 harmful batches unflagged. Overall coverage is 0.695,
below the nominal .95 target. Conditional-only change is an intentional failure case. Integration preserves the
calibrated-AC estimate exactly and does not create a more accurate estimator.
Contribution and boundary
The contribution is a reproducible, label-safe implementation and controlled comparison. It is not evidence of causal
explanation, successful adaptation, improved human decisions or real-world transfer. The implementation follows the
submitted Phase 2 DOCX, narrowed by the previously prepared Phase 3 plan. No Phase 2 instructor feedback was
received.
Reading the evidence
Online evidence contains no current realised accuracy or F1. Label verification is recorded at its release clock.
Evaluator-only files contain eventual truth for retrospective scoring. A missing concern flag is unresolved evidence, not a
verdict of safety. The offline viewer filters truth by the selected as-of clock.

1. Dataset, setup and frozen model
Each seed supplies disjoint samples of 4,000 training rows, 2,000 correctness-calibration rows, 2,000 historical reference
rows and 12,000 monitoring rows per condition. The 24 monitoring batches contain 500 rows each. Development seeds
are 90001-90005; the final seeds are 100-129. No final seed was replaced, dropped or selected from outcomes.
Conditions share Gaussian innovations and label uniforms within seed, creating pairing rather than extra replications.
Eight baseline inputs are generated from independent standard-normal innovations Z. Set X0=Z0, X1=.3 Z0+sqrt(1-.3^2)
Z1, and retain the other coordinates. Given the modified inputs, sample Y using p=sigmoid(1.2 X0-.9 X1+.6 X2). All values
are finite numeric data; no imputation or one-hot encoding is required. This is a transparent controlled generator, not a
reproduction of STAGGER, Agrawal or the reviewed Gaussian-mixture experiments.
ID Condition Intervention

C1 Nuisance mean X7 + 1.5 after row 4000
C2 Predictive mean X0 ramp +0 to +1.5, rows 4001-6000
C3 Abrupt conditional p becomes 1-p after row 4000

StandardScaler, LogisticRegression(C=1, max_iter=500), three-component PCA and MiniBatchKMeans(k=3, n_init=3,
batch_size=256) are fitted only on training rows. The logistic model and all representations are frozen. Correctness
isotonic regression is fitted only on calibration rows, using raw confidence max(p_model,1-p_model) and the original
model's correctness indicator. Class decisions remain p_model >= .5 after calibration. An ATC-style threshold is selected
on the same calibration partition. The complete 2,000-row reference supplies historical realised accuracy; its final 1,000
inputs are the fixed input-test reference.
For C6, X1=.9 Z0+sqrt(1-.9^2) Z1 after onset, so Var(X1)=.9^2+(1-.9^2)=1 and Cov(X0,X1)=.9. This fixes the legacy
correlated-coordinate rotation, which changed marginal variances. C6 has no changed marginal features. A dependence
change can still change the distribution of a linear PCA coordinate.
Hardware: i7-10610U laptop, approximately 32 GB RAM. The scientific environment is Anaconda Python 3.13.5, NumPy
2.1.3, SciPy 1.17.0, scikit-learn 1.8.0, pandas 2.3.3 and matplotlib 3.10.8. Numerical thread pools are limited to one. No
external dataset download or GPU is required.

2. Monitoring, targets and clock contract
For each batch, compute two-sided KS tests on eight original inputs and three frozen PCA coordinates. Holm correction at
.05 is applied to the 11-test family. An alarm means at least one rejection. The original-only ablation recomputes Holm
over eight tests at the same level; its multiplicity allocation is therefore different. Per-batch control is not a lifetime
false-alarm guarantee. SciPy method="auto" is used and warnings are recorded.
Feature evidence ranks the eight marginal KS distances. Ranking alone does not flag a feature; flags require a rejection
in the declared family. Three frozen clusters show sample counts, occupancy and mean estimated correctness. Cluster
rates below 20 observations are suppressed. Cluster summaries are descriptive, with no generating-region truth or
cluster-localization accuracy claim.
For confidence s_i=max(p_i,1-p_i), the frozen isotonic correctness map gives q_i=g(s_i). Estimated accuracy is
mean(q_i). A dynamic program computes the complete distribution of K=sum_i Bernoulli(q_i): P_j(k)=(1-q_j)P_(j-1)(k)+q_j
P_(j-1)(k-1). The .025 and .975 quantiles divided by 500 form a plug-in prediction interval for realised batch accuracy.
Independence and valid target correctness probabilities are required. Calibration-parameter uncertainty is omitted.
Historical accuracy A_ref is one observed reference proportion. The verification target is H=1{A_ref-A_batch>.05}; this is
realised deterioration, not certified expected-population harm. Concern is P_plugin(K/500 < A_ref-.05); a score >=.95
flags high proxy concern. Its integer cutoff is evaluated using exact rational arithmetic. The point ablation instead flags
A_ref-A_hat>.05. The concern score is not a calibrated probability certificate of harm under arbitrary shift.
Information flow
Training features and labels -> frozen artifacts -> current features and IDs -> persisted predictions and online evidence ->
scheduled released labels -> verified accuracy, macro-F1 and harm -> retrospective evaluator.
At input-completion clock t, prediction persistence is phase 0 and due label verification is phase 1. Labels for cohort t
become available at t+d. Delay zero still predicts before observing labels. After input batch 24, the clock drains through
24+d without inventing input batches. Prediction and label IDs must agree exactly. Feature-only scoring accepts neither
labels nor a broker handle. The broker is an API boundary, not a cryptographic sandbox.
Matched comparators
Calibrated AC alone is an identity baseline for IDM's impact channel. Raw AC removes calibration while preserving
classes. ATC-style estimation freezes a raw-confidence threshold matching calibration accuracy, with ties toward the
larger threshold. The historical-accuracy constant is a no-change baseline. Original-feature KS is evaluated natively as an
input detector and separately as a harm proxy. Released-label verification is an identity check available only after release,
not a perfect early predictor.

8. Relation to research and methodological changes
Kivimaki et al. [1] analyze AC and Poisson-binomial accuracy intervals under calibration, using synthetic
binary-classification experiments under covariate shift. We retain that estimator, freeze a correctness-calibration map
without changing predictions, and evaluate conditional-only failures explicitly. Our generator, feature count, learner grid
and sampling protocol differ, so published scores are not a comparable leaderboard. AC alone equals our impact channel;
integration is the contribution, not a novel accuracy estimator.
Garg et al. [2] propose threshold-based performance prediction. Our ATC-style comparator matches calibration accuracy
with a frozen raw-confidence threshold; its candidate endpoints and tie rule are explicit. This is an adaptation, not a
complete reproduction of the original experiments. Greco et al. [3] monitor deep representations; our PCA plus coordinate
KS procedure is not DriftLens or its Frechet-distance method.
SciPy's KS documentation [4] describes a two-sample continuous-distribution test and automatic exact/asymptotic
selection. Scikit-learn guidance [5] motivates evaluating calibration separately. Installed versions, recorded in
environment.json, govern execution. We make no numerical superiority claim over unreproduced literature methods and
do not repeat Phase 2's absolute novelty assertion.

9. Validation, cost and reproduction
The final run passed validation for 210 streams, 5,040 cohorts and 2,520,000 instance-level joins. All queues drained;
saved arrays verify calibrated-AC identity, delay invariance and absence of online truth columns. An independent
complete 30-seed reproduction matched exactly across 21 scientific CSVs and 2,400 arrays (33,010,080 values); elapsed
timings were excluded. The saved test log records 39 passing tests. The browser audit passed 488,880 release-boundary
checks. These checks establish implementation consistency, not every scientific assumption.

Reproduction
From the repository root, use the documented Anaconda executable. Run the scientific tests, create a fresh final output
directory, evaluate its CSVs, then build the figures and report. The supplied README includes exact PowerShell
commands and a full independent-reproduction comparison. Existing output directories are refused rather than
overwritten. The locked manifest, source hashes, package versions and complete seed vectors accompany the data.
Seeds and stage namespaces regenerate features; cached prediction arrays and evaluator labels enable audits.
Remaining limitations
Only one well-specified logistic task and one learner are evaluated. Calibration can decay under both covariate and
conditional shift. The PCA anchor can miss dependence directions; KS ranks are not causes. The .05 historical margin is
an observed-reference target and the .95 concern threshold is not a validated harm probability. There is no online
adaptation, selective-label analysis, external domain validation, causal evaluation or human study. Delay creates an
evidence gap and does not repair the confidence estimator. Implementation and integrity evidence address a narrowed
Phase 3 scope, not the entire original proposal.
