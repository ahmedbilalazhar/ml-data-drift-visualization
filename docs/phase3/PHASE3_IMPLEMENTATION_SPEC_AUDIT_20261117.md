## Audit findings that remain open

- **RQ3 remains unanswered.** A coordinated prototype is not evidence of analyst benefit. No analyst decision-effect experiment or human-benefit claim is reported.
- **Causal feature attribution remains unsupported.** Descriptive feature rankings and dependence evidence do not identify causes.
- **Localization is measured, but regional recovery is weak.** The follow-up reports 30-seed historical-only mappings, cluster composition/entropy, many-to-one region assignment, and region-2 ranking. At five clusters, full-space macro-F1 is 0.300 (95% seed-bootstrap CI 0.281–0.320), region-2 AP is 0.213 against roughly 0.20 prevalence, and ARI is 0.087 (0.078–0.096). Frozen PCA-2/PCA-3 do not materially improve macro-F1. This is a negative result for the tested cluster summaries, not proof that all localization is impossible.
- **Named literature baselines remain partly unevaluated.** Canonical River ADWIN is now evaluated inside the delayed and budgeted cohort protocol, but its strict-cap arm deadlocks after one probe and its exact-quota arm is constrained by forced acquisitions and sparse feedback. DriftLens and Domino remain unreproduced; no superiority claim against either is supported.
- **The total evidence-registry migration is incomplete.** This new registry is an audit source, but historical reports have not all been regenerated from it.
- **Matched adaptation matrix is broader but remains incomplete.** A fresh 30-seed sensitivity now crosses all three synthetic task families with stationary, gradual, recurrent-reversal and out-of-support mechanisms and delays 0/5/20 under matched clocks/feedback windows. It still uses one logistic classifier, one three-cluster partition, simulated data and unequal model capacity/compute between global and local arms. Natural-stream adaptation, threshold variants, alternative cluster counts and matched compute/cost are not evaluated. The audit-policy matrix covers its declared six-policy × two-cost-mode × three-budget × four-delay product, but not acquisition prices, randomized exploration on this stream, prospective deployment or every Phase 2 policy concept.
- **External transfer claims remain narrow.** NOAA is one station and one chronological holdout; same-day predictors do not provide a forecast. Existing UCI/ELEC2 limitations remain in their own reports.
- **Dataset integrity repair.** The whole-project audit initially found Elec2 and Covertype Parquet read errors. New `verified_v2` files have now been rebuilt from the exact checksummed raw sources, read back successfully, and installed as the paths used by the loader. The original unreadable Parquet files remain preserved as historical artifacts. The current whole-project audit passes all three admitted stream checksums, schemas, row counts, missingness and stable IDs.
- **Uncertainty and selective-label limits remain.** NOAA reference/calibrator uncertainty has a conditional 30-day moving-block sensitivity analysis, with the classifier held fixed; a separate training-block refit sensitivity is now measured, but remains conditional on one station and does not represent model misspecification, future regimes, or joint uncertainty guarantees. The probability-sampled selective-label experiments now include both synthetic streams and a retrospective NOAA replay on one fixed finite chronology. Prospective acquisition, independent-domain uncertainty, nonresponse, changing inclusion probabilities and selected-label recalibration remain unestablished.

## Capacity-matched adaptation control (18 November 2026)

A corrected follow-up isolates the cluster-conditional function class from the earlier local-vs-global capacity confound. The invalid first run used different initialization and is explicitly superseded by `adaptation_capacity_control_20261118_v2`; it is not used for claims. In v2, all arms start from the same historical global classifier. The local experts and pooled cluster-interaction logistic model have the same 27-dimensional intercept/slope function class and share four update clocks, released feedback IDs, 2,000-row per-update windows, and an 8,000 cumulative repeated-row-use cap.

The 30-seed matrix spans three synthetic tasks, four mechanisms, three delays and four arms: 4,320 cells, 414,720 persisted forecasts, and 21,600 fit-audit rows. The seed bank is intentionally reused from the immediately preceding adaptation-diversity extension so conditions can be compared in a paired way; it is not an independent replication. Feedback-ID, release-clock, update and hash contracts pass. Local versus pooled interaction mean accuracy differs by 0.0016 percentage points when averaged across settings; the interaction arm exceeds the single-global refit by 1.4413 points in the same seed-weighted summary. Local and interaction outcomes are therefore practically very close in this matrix. This points to cluster-conditional representation/capacity as a plausible explanation for the observed local-global gap; it does not establish that the monitoring or trigger policy caused it. Stationary gains in the conditional model, slight linear-task losses, 549 local fallback fits, different optimization cost, a single fixed clustering, and synthetic-only evaluation constrain interpretation. The report and figures are in `reports/phase3/adaptation_capacity_control_20261118_v9/`; the machine-readable requirement is `ADAPT-CAPACITY-01`.

A 20,000-resample paired max-t bootstrap adjusts the local-versus-global figure simultaneously across the 36 task/mechanism/delay contrasts; 32/36 simultaneous intervals exclude zero. The CSV retains the original pointwise intervals as well. The full suite result is **210 passed**. The final report v9 is a visually inspected two-page TeX/PDF artifact.
