# IDM-Viz Phase 3 weakness disposition - 16 November 2026

This register updates the Phase 2 traceability and existing v5 report with the 16 November extensions. It is not a claim that every submitted Phase 2 ambition is complete. The submitted Phase 2 DOCX, implementation brief and pre-deadline archive remain byte-for-byte preserved. No instructor feedback was received.

## Unresolved weaknesses and why the evidence is insufficient

| Weakness | Current status | Evidence required for a valid resolution |
|---|---|---|
| Causal feature graph, causal drift type or responsibility | Not implemented. KS rankings, clusters and max-correlation pairs are descriptive | A defensible causal identification strategy or designed interventions with validated structural assumptions. A feature alarm alone cannot identify cause |
| Decision effect of coordinated views | RQ3 remains unanswered; the view is an implemented prototype | No decision-effect evidence is measured in this computational evaluation |
| General future-harm prediction | Prospective five-batch forecast tested on known synthetic laws and one manually designed withheld conditional-randomization law; aligned-onset confound exposed and followed by balanced-onset evaluation | Independent natural domains and multiple genuinely distinct unseen intervention families; calibrated forecast-interval coverage under shift; validation split by domain/time |
| Accuracy identification under arbitrary conditional shift | Fundamentally not identifiable from unlabeled inputs alone; Gas Sensor and ELEC2 outcomes expose transfer failures | Explicit assumptions, prospective released labels or a valid domain adaptation design. More seeds or different plots cannot solve identifiability |
| Calibrated harm probability or certified population bound | Concern score is a plug-in Poisson-binomial probability with q treated as known | Coverage studies that include calibrator/reference uncertainty, dependence and shift; an explicit target population and valid sampling assumptions |
| Selective-label bias correction and operational audit usefulness | Equal-label-budget audit allocation is evaluated synthetically; selected labels do not validate nonselected groups | A prospective sampling design with known inclusion probabilities, positivity checks, estimator comparison and realistic acquisition costs |
| Faithful named literature baselines | KS/PCA, ATC-style, persistence and internal baselines exist; named DriftLens/Domino/ADWIN-U/benchmark methods are not reproduced | Protocol-fidelity implementation, same data splits and label budgets, verified settings, and source-backed numerical comparisons. Simplified detectors must be labeled as inspired/style baselines |
| Human, financial and deployment cost | Only row-label budgets and limited runtime are reported | Real operational measurement; cost and workload cannot be inferred from synthetic CPU timing |
| Broad adaptation comparison | A cumulative logistic refit study and recalibration diagnostic exist, but they are selected scenarios and have unequal information histories | Multiple adaptation policies under common data, label, compute and retraining budgets, across independent streams and delays |

## Current scientific claim boundary

The strengthened artifact supports a bounded claim: IDM-Viz now has label-safe feature monitoring, descriptive localization, calibrated current-cohort correctness estimates, exact plug-in impact intervals, delayed verification, a coordinated view, controlled synthetic evidence, two external-data exercises, and reproducible sensitivity analyses. The evidence also shows failures: dependence sensitivity costs alarm burden, external calibration can fail severely, exact intervals do not repair it, and delayed isotonic recalibration did not reduce estimation MAE on the tested ELEC2 sequence. No causal, decision-benefit, future-risk-certification or broad-transfer claim is supported.
